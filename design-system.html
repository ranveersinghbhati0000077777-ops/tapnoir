02 — Typography

Power sans for the statement · serif italic for the feeling
Display · Sora 700 Every
connection Uppercase headlines, tracking −3.5%, line-height 0.92–0.96. Sizes 96 / 72 / 48 / 32.
Editorial · Cormorant Garamond Italic without limits. One phrase per headline, ~118% of the sans size. Never for body or UI.
Body & UI · Manrope A premium smart business card. Your identity, one tap away — shared in a single, effortless gesture. Label · 12 / 0.3em tracking Body 18 / 16 / 14, line-height 1.65. Labels uppercase, widely tracked.

04 — Do

Cinematic studio light, deep natural shadows · matte black, brushed metal, champagne finishes · generous whitespace · one idea per section · slow, subtle motion (600–1100ms ease-out).

05 — Avoid

Blue/purple gradients · rounded SaaS cards · icon grids · cartoon illustration · gold fills or glowing gold effects · stock imagery · crowded sections.
